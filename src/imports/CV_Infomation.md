In this project, I spearheaded the foundational Salesforce configuration for **Mzansi GreenTech Solutions**, a renewable energy company expanding across Southern Africa. My primary objective was to align their Salesforce environment with a complex business model that requires seamless collaboration between the Sales and Operations departments. To achieve this, I established a comprehensive organizational setup including custom branding, localized settings for the Johannesburg headquarters, and a standard fiscal year tailored to their May-April financial cycle.

The final delivery resulted in a professional, scalable CRM that resolved existing challenges like unstructured org settings and cross-departmental confusion. By standardizing record creation and implementing automated sharing rules, I created a transparent environment where Sales can track project progress and Operations can access vital client history. This setup not only improved current operational efficiency but also provided the necessary infrastructure for the company’s future expansion into Botswana and Namibia.
